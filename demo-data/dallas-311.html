Dallas 311: reporting and routine potholes

Source for this section: City of Dallas 311 Frequently Asked Questions.

How do I submit a Dallas service request?

Use phone, the Dallas 311 app or the official portal, rather than email.

What if the Dallas 311 app fails?

Report the app problem to dallas311@dallas.gov or call 311 / 214-670-3111.

Where is Dallas department guidance?

The official portal's Knowledge tab has searchable articles and service-request links.

What happens after a routine pothole report?

Public Works inspects nonhazardous potholes. A qualifying pothole is assigned to a crew within three business days, subject to staffing, weather and closures. Larger damage goes to routine street repair. Crew assignment is not repair completion.

What if a Dallas pothole is hazardous?

Call 311.

Does Dallas repair private parking-lot potholes?

Private-property and parking-lot potholes are the property or business owner's responsibility.

Will a street repair report replace the road?

Inspection determines the appropriate response; a report does not guarantee resurfacing.

What if Dallas 311 Knowledge is incorrect?

Report outdated or incorrect articles to dallas311@dallas.gov.

Dallas street hazards: telephone reporting

Source for this section: Dallas 311 streets service directory.

Can I use a routine online report for every street problem?

No. Dallas lists hazardous potholes, emergency flooding, hazardous drainage, missing or damaged hazardous inlet lids, and emergency fallen trees or low limbs among issues to report by calling 311. The directory separately lists routine pothole and routine street repair services. Keep hazardous reporting separate from the routine demo request.

Dallas water: emergency, billing and permit contacts

Source for this section: Dallas Water Utilities contacts.

Where do I report a broken water main or leaking hydrant?

For water and sewer emergencies, call 311, or 214-670-3111 from outside Dallas city limits. This reporting route is available 24 hours a day. Listed issues include water-main breaks, leaking meters or hydrants, clogged or overflowing wastewater mains, and emergency water shutoff.

Who handles a Dallas water bill, new account or service change?

Call Water Customer Service at 214-651-1441, Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., or email WaterSpecialtyUnit@dallas.gov. The emergency reporting hours above do not describe billing support hours.

Who handles water or sewer permits and main availability?

Call the Dallas Water Utilities Permit Section at 214-948-4480, option 4, Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.

Dallas traffic signals and streetlight outages

Source for this section: Dallas Traffic Signals and Street Lighting.

Is a flashing or dark intersection the same as a streetlight outage?

No. Report an intersection whose signals are flashing or dark by calling 311 and speaking directly with an agent. A streetlight outage has a different reporting route.

How do I report a Dallas streetlight outage?

Use the Oncor streetlight reporting website. If the light is missing from that website, report it through Dallas 311 by phone, official portal or app.

What if I already reported the streetlight and it remains unrepaired?

The Dallas page directs reports of lights still unrepaired after 15 days to the Dallas 311 portal for City staff follow-up. This reference does not show the status of a particular repair.

What this demo reference can confirm
