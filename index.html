What is the return policy?+

For this fictional demo, unopened, non-personalized products can be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery with proof of purchase. Personalized products and opened hygiene products are excluded. A human representative may review unusual circumstances.

How long does standard delivery take?+

In this fictional demo, standard delivery is estimated at 3 to 5 business days after dispatch, excluding weekends and public holidays. This is an estimate, not a guarantee.

Can I check or change an order?+

This demonstration is not connected to a live order system. It cannot check order status, change an order, submit a return, or issue a refund.

Is Cedar Market a real store?+

No. Cedar Market, its policies, and this website are fictional and created for a customer experience demonstration. Please do not submit personal or payment information.
