What is the return policy?+

Unopened, non-personalized products can be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery with proof of purchase. Personalized products and opened hygiene products are excluded. A human representative may review unusual circumstances.

How long does standard delivery take?+

Standard delivery is estimated at 3 to 5 business days after dispatch, excluding weekends and public holidays. This is an estimate, not a guarantee.

Can I check or change an order?+

Our assistant can help you choose flowers, confirm a quote and check your flower order using its five-digit number. For other order changes, returns or refunds, ask for a customer care representative. This website prepares requests and does not submit orders.

Is Cedar Market a real store?+

No. Cedar Market, its policies, and this website are fictional and created for a customer experience demonstration. Please do not submit personal or payment information.
